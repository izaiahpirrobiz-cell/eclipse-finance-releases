Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

Eclipse Finance keeps your books in a file on your own computer. This page describes the few things that ever leave it, and what happens to them.

The short version

Your transactions, balances, invoices, customers and receipts stay on your machine. We do not collect them, cannot read them, and have no server holding them. There is no analytics or tracking in the app. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture and nothing else.

Who this is

Eclipse Finance is made by Eclipse Publishing Studios. If you want to reach a person about anything on this page, use the support page.

Where your books live

In a database file in your user profile directory, on the computer you installed the app on. It is created the first time you make an account in the app, and it never moves unless you move it.

The password you set is used to derive the key that opens that file. It is not stored anywhere — only a verifier that can check a password is correct without revealing it. This means nobody can reset it for you. The app issues a recovery code when you create the account, and that code is the only way back in if the password is lost.

Receipts and documents you attach are copied into the same profile directory and stay there.

Everything that leaves your computer

The complete list, so you can check it against what the app actually does.

Checking for updates
The app asks GitHub whether a newer version has been published. GitHub sees the request the way any website sees a visitor — your IP address and the fact that a request was made. Nothing about you or your books is included.
Signing in, if you choose to
Sign-in is optional and the app works fully without it. If you use it, the sign-in happens in your own web browser at Google, not inside the app — the app never sees your Google password. We receive your account identifier, email address, display name and profile picture, and use them only to show who is signed in. Google's handling of that sign-in is covered by Google's privacy policy.
Payment processors, if you connect one
Only if you connect Stripe, Square or PayPal yourself. The app then talks to that processor to fetch your own transactions. Your keys are held in your operating system's keychain, never in the app's own files, and never sent to us.

And nothing else

No analytics. No crash reporting. No telemetry. No advertising identifiers. No third-party trackers of any kind. The app makes no network request that is not in the list above.

What we store about you

If you never sign in: nothing. We hold no record that you exist.

If you do sign in, we store the account identifier, email address, display name and profile picture that Google provides, using Google Firebase. That is the whole record. We do not receive or store your Google password, your contacts, your files, or anything else from your Google account.

Cloud backup, when it exists

An optional cloud copy of your books is planned and is not built yet. When it arrives it will work like this, and this page will be updated with the detail before it ships rather than after:

  • Your books are encrypted on your machine, with a passphrase we never receive, before anything is uploaded.
  • What sits in storage is therefore unreadable to us and to the hosting provider.
  • Deleting a cloud copy deletes it, rather than hiding it behind a flag.
  • It is optional. The app will keep working entirely offline without it.

The consequence of real end-to-end encryption is worth stating plainly: if you lose that passphrase, we cannot recover the cloud copy for you. That is what makes the guarantee meaningful.

Children

Eclipse Finance is a bookkeeping tool for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Your choices

  • Use it without an account. Sign-in is optional and always will be while your books are on your own disk.
  • Export everything. Every table and report exports to CSV from inside the app, so your data is not trapped in it.
  • Delete everything. Removing the app's profile directory removes your books. If you signed in and want that record deleted, ask through the support page and we will remove it.
  • Sign out. Signing out removes the stored session from your machine and leaves your books exactly where they are.

Changes to this page

If what the app does changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top changes too. Anything that would newly send information off your machine will be described here before it ships, not afterwards.
